Asset type: services page image for MAKERS3D, landscape 4:3 composition.
Style: premium photorealistic studio product photography with realistic fine fabrication details and subtle 3D print layer texture on polymer parts. Understated contemporary editorial aesthetic.
Composition: three-quarter view, entire object or compact group fully visible in frame, centered, balanced, generous clear margins around all edges. Physical tabletop-scale objects, no collage panels or illustration labels.
Background: clean isolated object cutout with genuine transparent alpha. The asset will be displayed on pure white #FFFFFF. No opaque tabletop, grey or cream backdrop, vignette, decorative gradient, horizon line, broad drop shadows or glow. Shading on the objects themselves only.
Constraints: no people, hands, writing, logos, brands, watermarks, text overlays, computer screens or CAD drawings. No cropped parts.
Subject: a coherent group that conveys an in-house design, 3D printing, finishing and production studio: a compact precise black-and-silver open-frame 3D printer with a light-grey enclosure on its build plate, a small expertly finished blue-and-white miniature mobile crane model to one side, three identical matte-grey printed mounting brackets neatly aligned in front, and one small elegant white ribbed sculptural vase. Compact arrangement with the printer behind and the finished objects in front, all at believable tabletop scale. No excavator or JCB. Clear quiet composition rather than a busy workshop.
